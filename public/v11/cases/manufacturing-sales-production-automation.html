Case 06 · Automation

Automating Sales & Production Workflow for a Manufacturing Company

One CRM that connects lead capture, the sales pipeline and production jobs.

Sector
Manufacturing
Focus
Automation
01Unified platform
03+Lead sources

The challenge

Leads, sales and order processing lived in separate tools, which caused delays and limited visibility.

Who it is for

Manufacturers that run leads, sales and order processing in disconnected tools.

What we built

A custom CRM that centralizes lead capture, automates follow-ups and sales workflows, and turns confirmed orders into production jobs in one platform.

  • 01Automated lead capture
  • 02End-to-end CRM workflow
  • 03Sales and production sync

What changed

  • Full visibility of the sales funnel, follow-ups and conversions
  • Fewer manual handoffs between sales and production
  • Faster response times and better pipeline management
  • More efficient sales and manufacturing teams
  • A CRM that scales with the business
