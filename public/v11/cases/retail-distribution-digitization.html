Case 05 · Digital transformation

Digitizing a Major Mobile Brand's Retail & Distribution Ecosystem

A central mobile platform for real-time sales tracking, schemes and channel operations.

Sector
Consumer brands and retail
Focus
Digital transformation
01Unified platform
04Stakeholder roles

The challenge

Retail and distribution operations ran on manual reporting, which meant limited visibility, delayed payouts and inefficient channel management.

Who it is for

Consumer brands with retailers, distributors and field sales teams.

What we built

A central, role-based mobile app that digitized sales, schemes and incentive tracking, with real-time analytics, reward redemption and dashboards for every channel stakeholder.

  • 01Role-based dashboards
  • 02Real-time analytics
  • 03Instant redemption
  • 04Unified platform

What changed

  • Manual Excel processes removed across retail and distribution
  • Real-time visibility into sales, schemes and incentives
  • Faster payouts and reward redemption across the channel
  • More transparency between retailers, distributors and sales teams
  • A scalable digital platform the brand can build on
